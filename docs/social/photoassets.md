# The 20 pictures at intus.fit/photoassets

These are the pictures Buffer posts to Instagram and X. They are square PNGs, 2160 by 2160, served at `https://www.intus.fit/photoassets/<file>` from `public/photoassets/`. The page at `/photoassets` lists them and asks not to be indexed.

## What changed on 29 Oct 2026 (version 3)

All 20 pictures were redrawn and the copy was rewritten. The file names did not change, so every existing link still works, but **the file name no longer says what the picture shows**. Read the table below, not the name.

- The small label above the headline (for example "FILE 001", "QUESTION") was removed from every picture.
- Nine pictures are red and eleven are paper (light ground, ink text). Version 2 was all red.
- The copy of the old pictures 14 and 18 ("Pay once. Keep it. Forever." and "You already pay for the tracker, the food and the tests.") is gone, because it broke the no-pricing rule in `facts.md`. Pictures 16 and 20 were reworded to say only things in `facts.md`.
- Every picture now says one thing that is true of the product today, or asks a plain question. The fact ids below are the ones to cite. "Framing" means the picture makes no claim about the product.

Pictures 11 and 12 were replaced again on 29 Oct 2026 ("Wellness, consolidated." and "A quiet record of your health."), after the first version 3 upload. They were "A personal health tracker." and "Everything you track. One quiet record.".

If a Buffer post still says something that matched the old picture, change the post, not the picture.

## Rules for using them

- Use a picture only with a caption that fits what the picture says now. Do not reuse a caption written for the old picture of the same file name.
- Every caption must still pass `facts.md` and `voice.md`. The only cost wording is "Free during early access." (picture 20 says exactly that).
- Alt text describes the picture as it is: ground colour, the headline in quotes, any diagram, "Intus mark top left, INTUS.FIT bottom left".
- Cache note: Buffer and X keep their own copy of a picture once a post is scheduled. After a picture changes, edit the scheduled post and attach the picture again, or the old one may still go out.
